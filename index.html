01 / THE BUSINESS NEED
THE CONTROL GAP

AI agents can act at machine speed.
Enterprise controls still move at human speed.

01

Authorization is fragmented

Agent identity, user intent and payment approval live in separate systems. Merchants cannot reliably tell what an agent was allowed to buy.

02

Gateways lack commerce context

AI gateways route model and tool traffic, but they do not enforce price, payee, currency, delegation or transaction-specific mandates.

03

Evidence breaks at handoffs

Teams cannot follow one transaction across the agent, AI gateway, payment processor, merchant order and fulfillment lifecycle.

02 / THE SOLUTION
A NEUTRAL ENFORCEMENT LAYER

Agentic Commerce Gateway
One Control point for Agentic
transactions across all your gateways.

Agentic Commerce Gateway sits beside the AI gateway—not inside the merchant storefront. It validates agent identity and commerce mandates, applies deterministic enterprise policy, coordinates payment and order execution, and writes linked evidence for every decision.
